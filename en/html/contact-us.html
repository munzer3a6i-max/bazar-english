FAQ

Now showing questions:
Orders & Shipping
How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by SMS to your mobile number. You can also follow your order status from the "My Orders" page in your account.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery arrives within 2–3 business days in Riyadh and Jeddah, and can take up to 5 business days for the rest of the Kingdom. Express delivery is available within one business day in major cities.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

You can modify or cancel your order within one hour of confirmation from the "My Orders" page, as long as it hasn't already shipped. After shipping, please contact our support team for assistance.

Returns & Refunds
How do I request a product return?

From the "My Orders" page, select the order, then click "Request Return" and choose the reason for the return. You'll receive a free shipping label to return the product within 14 days of receipt.

When will I receive my refund?

After the returned product is received and inspected, the refund is issued within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

Can I exchange the product instead of returning it?

Yes, you can request an exchange for the same available size or color from the same order page, with no extra fees.

Payment & Billing
What payment methods are available?

We accept mada, Visa, Mastercard, and Apple Pay, as well as installment options through Tamara, Tabby, and Madfoo3, and cash on delivery.

How do I get a tax invoice?

The tax invoice is automatically sent to your email as soon as the order is confirmed, and you can also download it from the order details page in your account.

Why did my payment fail?

This can happen due to insufficient balance, incorrect card details, or a decline from the issuing bank. Make sure your details are correct or try another payment method, and if the issue persists, contact our support team.

Availability by City
Is delivery available to all cities in the Kingdom?

Yes, we deliver to all regions of the Kingdom. Express delivery (within one business day) is currently available in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam, while other cities receive orders within 3-5 business days.

How do I know if a specific product is available in my city?

Select your city from the top of the page under "Deliver to", and the products available for shipping to your area will automatically be shown, along with the estimated arrival date.

Can I change the delivery city after placing my order?

You can update your delivery address before your order ships from the "My Orders" page. After shipping, please contact our support team for help with redirecting it.

Stores & Sellers
How do I open a store on Saudi Bazaar?

You can apply through the "Become a Seller" page, and our registration team will contact you within 3-5 business days after reviewing your commercial registration and documents.

Who is responsible for product quality, the seller or the platform?

All sellers on the platform are approved and held to strict quality standards. If there's an issue with a product, the Saudi Bazaar team steps in to ensure a fair resolution for the customer.

How do I contact a seller about a specific product?

You can contact the seller directly using the "Contact Seller" button on the product page, or through your order details page after purchase.

Account & Security
How do I reset my password?

From the sign-in page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your mobile number. We'll send you a verification code to reset your password.

How do I update my personal information?

You can update your name, mobile number, email, and addresses from the "Account Settings" page at any time.

Is my personal and payment information protected?

Yes, all data is encrypted to the highest security standards, and we do not store your bank card details on our servers.
